# Controls:

TLDR: Click to select things or advance. You might need to enter and exit areas for it to pick up. The controls for each part are explained in game when you need it, this is just for reference.



For Visual Novel Parts:

- Click or press E to advance.

- You can also press W to display all the text at once, if you want to speedrun it for some reason.



For Comic Parts:

- Press E, or click to advance.



For Pick What's Wrongs:

- Use your mouse to move, and click to select. You might need to enter and exit the object for it to pick up (Sorry about that!)



For Detective Parts:

- Use your mouse to move, and click on things to select them. Might need to do the same as above if the issue occurs

- To travel between scenes, bring your mouse to the top corners and it'll do it. You could do anywhere but that might trigger the dialogue a bit early!

- Talk to the Llama when you have all the evidence to advance



For the Thinking Game:

- Click or Press to jump.

- Follow what you think happened to go on the right path!
